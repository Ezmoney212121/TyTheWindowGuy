Common Questions

Window Cleaning FAQ — Bend & Central Oregon

Straight answers about pricing, frequency, what’s included, and how we work.

How much does window cleaning cost in Bend, Oregon?

Pricing depends on the number of windows, how accessible they are, and whether you need interior, exterior, or both. Most homes receive a free, no-obligation quote so you know the exact price before we schedule. Call (541) 633-8728 or use the quote form below.

How often should I have my windows cleaned in Central Oregon?

Most Central Oregon homes look and feel best with professional cleaning 1–2 times per year. Homes near dusty roads, active construction, or with heavy pine pollen and needles may need service more often. Many customers book seasonal cleanings in spring and fall.

What is included in a residential window cleaning?

A standard residential service includes thorough cleaning of window glass (interior and/or exterior as requested) and careful attention to accessible sills and tracks. Screen cleaning, skylights, hard-water spot treatment, and post-construction cleanup can be added. We wear shoe covers and protect your property on every visit.

Do you clean commercial windows in Bend?

Yes. We provide commercial window cleaning for offices, retail storefronts, and other businesses throughout Bend and Central Oregon. We work around your schedule to keep disruption low and storefronts looking sharp.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Bend and Central Oregon, including Sunriver, Redmond, Sisters, Tumalo, Terrebonne, and surrounding communities. If you’re nearby and unsure, call — we’re happy to confirm coverage for your address.

Are you insured?

Yes. Ty The Window Guy is fully insured for your peace of mind. We treat every home and business with care, including shoe covers indoors and careful work around landscaping and exterior surfaces.

Do you clean screens, skylights, gutters, and roof debris?

Yes. In addition to interior and exterior window cleaning, we offer screen cleaning, skylight cleaning, gutter cleaning, roof debris and pine needle clearing, and post-construction cleanups — services that are especially useful under Central Oregon’s ponderosa pines.
